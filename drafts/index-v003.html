XIAO Motion ML - v003

Collect, train, push and pull motion models for firmware-v003.ino from a phone or a desktop. The device link is WebBLE (Chrome on Android, desktop Chrome or Edge) or Web Serial (desktop Chrome or Edge); the phone's own motion sensor is a second data source. One file, no libraries. Not tested on real hardware.
