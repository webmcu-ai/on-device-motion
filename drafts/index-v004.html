XIAO Motion ML - v004

Collect, train, push and pull motion models for firmware-v003.ino from a phone or a desktop. The device link is WebBLE (Chrome on Android, desktop Chrome or Edge) or Web Serial (desktop Chrome or Edge); the phone's own motion sensor is a second data source. One file, no libraries. Or take the SD card out of the XIAO, put it in this computer and train on it directly (section 2). Not tested on real hardware.

2. SD card folder

Take the SD card out of the XIAO, put it in this computer and train on its data here. A browser cannot take a typed path, so press Choose SD folder and pick the card's top folder (the one holding motion and header); the chooser opens where you were last, and Reopen last folder picks the same folder again after a permission prompt. Writing to the card needs desktop Chrome or Edge; other browsers can read a folder, and Save then gives the SD-ready .zip.

no folder chosen

Reading: motion/<class>/*.csv samples, header/config.json (class names and layout), header/myCalib.bin and header/myMotionWeights.bin. Weights found become the starting point for training here and for inference (Analyze runs them on every sample, phone live inference uses them). Saving writes new samples as the next free sN.csv (existing files are never overwritten), config.json, and, when a model is in memory, myMotionWeights.bin, myCalib.bin and myMotionWeights.h together so weights and calibration stay in step. If Chrome refuses the card's top folder, copy motion and header into an ordinary folder, or choose motion and then use "Add motion or header folder" for header. Put the card back in the XIAO and reboot it to use the new weights.
